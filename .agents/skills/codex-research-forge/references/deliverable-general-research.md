# Deliverable: General Research Brief

Use this type when the research does not primarily feed one of the six
specialized deliverables: architecture study, ADR, technical documentation,
vendor comparison, business case, or compliance mapping. It fits broad
landscape surveys, evidence syntheses, historical or technical overviews,
and other scoped questions without a specialized report structure. If a
specific choice or system design is central, use the ADR, vendor comparison,
or architecture type instead.

## Question bank

Route answers into the slots of the interview protocol.

- Topic, context, purpose, and the decision or deliverable informed (slot 2).
- Scope boundaries: population, geography, period, systems, or concepts to
  include or exclude, as relevant (slots 3 and 7).
- The audience and assumed knowledge — required user-stated; no default
  (slot 6).
- Must-consult sources, source preferences, and source types to avoid
  (slot 4).
- Report structure, language, and numeric length target (slot 5).
- Recency window, where the topic is time-sensitive; propose options and
  require the user's approval (slot 7).

## Sub-question template

Draft only the questions that fit the topic. Each question must have its own
objective, output shape, source guidance when narrower than the global
criteria, and boundaries.

1. What is the scoped topic, baseline, or current state, and how should it be
   defined?
2. What evidence answers each key part of the research question?
3. Where do credible sources agree or disagree, and what explains the
   differences?
4. What implications follow for the user's stated purpose or decision?
5. What evidence gaps, uncertainties, or unanswered questions remain?

Use 3-6 bounded questions total; do not add a recommendation unless the
user's purpose calls for one.

## Output skeleton and length

Default sections, in order: executive summary; scope and definitions;
findings organized by research question; synthesis and implications for the
stated purpose; evidence gaps and open questions; sources.

Default length: 1500-2500 words. The user may choose another numeric target.

## Source preferences

Prefer primary sources, official documentation, original research, and
high-quality reviews or synthesis from credible institutions, selecting
among them based on the topic. Avoid SEO listicles, content farms, undated
or unattributed posts, and secondhand summaries when the primary source is
reachable.

For time-sensitive topics, propose a recency window suited to the field and
ask the user to approve it. Flag older foundational sources when they remain
relevant.

## Fixed clauses

- Distinguish documented facts from interpretation; state uncertainty and
  evidence gaps explicitly.
- Represent material disagreements between credible sources rather than
  choosing silently.
- Tie implications to the user's stated purpose; do not invent a decision
  or recommendation the user did not request.
