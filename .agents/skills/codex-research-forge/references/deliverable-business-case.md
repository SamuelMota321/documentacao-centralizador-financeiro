# Deliverable: Business Case

Research feeding an investment justification: adopt, buy, build, or expand —
argued to decision-makers in financial terms.

## When this type applies

The user must justify spending or strategic change and the deliverable
argues economics: problem cost, options, financials, risks. If the vendors
are already the question, use the vendor-comparison type; the two chain
naturally (business case first, vendor comparison after approval).

## Question bank

- The problem and its cost today — push for quantification: what does doing
  nothing cost per month or year? (slot 2)
- Options to analyze. Always force the do-nothing baseline into the list,
  and say so. (slot 3)
- Financial horizon and the metrics the organization uses: NPV, IRR,
  payback period, ROI — which of these does the decision-maker expect?
  (slot 5)
- Known inputs and assumptions: budget ranges, team costs, growth
  expectations. (slot 7)
- Risk appetite and known constraints. (slot 7)
- Audience default: executive decision-makers. (slot 6)

## Sub-question template

1. Evidence quantifying the problem and its current cost.
2. The option landscape, including the do-nothing baseline, with the
   characteristics of each option.
3. Costs and benefits per option over {horizon}, with every external number
   sourced and its assumptions stated.
4. Sensitivity of the result to the key assumptions — which changes flip
   the recommendation?
5. Risks per option and their mitigations.
6. Implementation feasibility of the leading option for an organization of
   the stated profile.

## Output skeleton and length

Sections, in order: one-page executive summary that leads with the
recommendation; quantified problem statement; options analysis including
do-nothing; financials with sensitivity ranges; risk assessment with
mitigations; recommendation tied to the financials.
Default length: 1500-2500 words.

## Source preferences

Prefer: primary market and pricing data, financial filings, vendor primary
material, analyst reports.
Recency default: pricing sources from the last 12 months; everything else
from the last 2 years; flag older ones.

## Fixed clauses

- Every external number carries a source and stated assumptions.
- The options analysis must include the do-nothing baseline.
- Lead with the recommendation; the detail follows it.
